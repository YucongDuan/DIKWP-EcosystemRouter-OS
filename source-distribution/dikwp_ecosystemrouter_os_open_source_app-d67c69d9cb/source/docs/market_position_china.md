# China Market Position

## Market thesis

China's AI market is moving from model demos to industry deployment. The decisive layer will be trust infrastructure: who is allowed to connect AI agents to industry workflows, hardware, sensitive data, evidence, authorization and audit.

## EcosystemRouter role

DIKWP EcosystemRouter OS is not an industry adapter. It is the meta-system that routes projects and partners into official DIKWP trust programs.

## Priority sectors

- AI+manufacturing
- trusted hardware and Xinchuang adaptation
- industrial agent governance
- government and state-owned enterprise AI pilots
- education and workforce transformation
- legal semantic space
- healthcare navigation
- traffic and city governance

## Why DIKWP fits

DIKWP provides a structured chain from data to purpose and reliability. That is useful for domains where output is not enough; the system must also expose intention, evidence, boundary, recovery and responsibility.
