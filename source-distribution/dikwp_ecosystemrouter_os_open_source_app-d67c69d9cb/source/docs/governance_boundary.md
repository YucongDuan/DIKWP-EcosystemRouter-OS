# Governance Boundary

This system must not be used to:

- falsely claim official approval
- impersonate Yucong Duan or official DIKWP status
- issue certification without governance process
- make legal conclusions about trademark or copyright
- hide attribution gaps
- threaten legal action without evidence and professional review

Allowed uses:

- portfolio strategy
- partner review
- registry readiness scoring
- attribution repair
- copycat risk communication
- commercial route planning
